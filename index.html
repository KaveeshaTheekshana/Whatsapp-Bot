FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything About bot.kaveesha.top

How do I link my WhatsApp account on bot.kaveesha.top?

Enter your WhatsApp number (07X or 947X format) on bot.kaveesha.top to generate an 8-digit Pairing Code, then open WhatsApp > Linked Devices > Link a device > Link with phone number instead and enter the code.

How does View-Once (One-View) saving work?

Send any reply (such as a dot, ok, or emoji) to a View-Once photo, video, or voice message before opening it. The bot immediately saves and delivers the full media privately to your WhatsApp You self-chat.

Can the bot recover messages deleted while I am offline?

Yes. Messages and media deleted while you are offline are securely captured in an encrypted buffer and automatically delivered to your You chat as soon as you come online.

Where do bot commands work and how do I clean my chat?

All commands (.info, .yt, .ytmp3, .fb, .fbmp3, .tik, .tikmp3, .gpoff, .gpon, .clean) work privately inside your own WhatsApp You self-chat. Send .clean anytime to clear bot text messages sent in the last 24 hours.
